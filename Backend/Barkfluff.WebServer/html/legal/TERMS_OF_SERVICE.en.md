## 3. The service

A BarkFluff node consists of backend microservices that the Windows, Android, macOS and web clients connect to. A node may provide a public website, the gRPC-Web proxy, the developer portal, file storage, realtime updates, push notifications, email notifications and a support chat — the exact set is decided by its administrator.

- **Account** — a user account with email, username, password, profile and devices. The account belongs to a specific node and is not valid on another node.
- **Content** — messages, files, images, avatars, posters, reactions, settings and other data you create or upload.
- **Client** — the BarkFluff application or web interface that uses the gRPC/gRPC-Web API.

## 8. What federation means for you

- Federation is disabled by default; the node administrator turns it on.
- Federated chats are 1-to-1 only. Group chats between nodes are not supported.
- **Federated messages are not end-to-end encrypted.** Their content is available both to your node and to your correspondent's node. Private and secret chats work within a single node only.
- By chatting with a user of another node you effectively hand your messages and files over to that node's administrator. Deleting them on your side does not delete their copy.
- Incoming federated private chats can be refused in the privacy settings.
- For details see the Privacy Policy, section "Federated data exchange".
